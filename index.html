Here's a guide to this relatively unintuitive system:

Quick Tips:

  • Bac: Typing the string of letters "bac" on any static page results in returning to the homepage.
  • S: Typing "S" while it is your turn in a game of scrabble results in the same thing as pressing the SWAP button does (albeit a little faster).
  • P: Typing "P" in a game of scrabble results in the same thing as pressing the PAUSE button does (albeit a little faster).

A Few Letter Word Tips:

  • You really don't need to memorize all of the words.
  • A ~40% set of words are what are used ~80% of the time.
  • I implore you all to print out the words and have them next to you when you are not playing competitively (of course).
  • Place an emphasis on learning the "clunky" (in my humble opinion) letters: "Q", "J", "Z", "X", "V", "K", "C", "U".
  • If there is a letter you see consistently on your rack that you just can't seem to get rid of, and have to make low scoring plays, learn about that letter.

Tips about Bingos:

  • A bingo is an event when a player uses all of the letters on their rack in a single turn. Due to this, they gain an extra 50 pts.
  • Bingos are very rare. I, myself have only seen 4 bingos being played over more than 100 games.
  • Bingos are most common (I would guess) early on in the game where there is a lot of open space.
  • If a bingo has not happened in the first 10 turns, there is a strong chance it will not occur.
  • ~81.65% of Bingos usually have an "S", "E", "D", "T", "R", "G", and "Y" as their last letter.
  • Keep an eye out for the suffixes "ing", "ST", "LY", "ER", "ERS", "ED", "S", and "IER".
  • Sometimes, no matter how "good" your rack seems, there might not be a bingo on it!
